Risk management is what separates investors who last from those who burn out quickly. Even the best analysis still needs capital protection.

## Deciding how much to stake

Before thinking about gains, define the risk. <Term slug="risk-reward">Risk Reward Ratio</Term> weighs the potential loss of a position against its potential gain. Many investors simply skip positions where the reward does not justify the risk.

<Term slug="position-sizing">Position Sizing</Term> determines how much capital goes into a single position, so one wrong call cannot damage the entire portfolio.

## Capping the downside

<Term slug="stop-loss">Stop Loss</Term> is a price level that closes a position when price moves against the plan. It turns an open ended loss into one you accounted for from the start.

<Term slug="diversification">Diversification</Term> spreads capital across several stocks or sectors, so a decline in one asset does not hit everything at once.

## Measuring resilience

Understand <Term slug="drawdown">Drawdown</Term> too, how far your portfolio can fall from its peak before recovering. Knowing that number helps you stay calm when markets turn volatile, and keeps decisions rational.
