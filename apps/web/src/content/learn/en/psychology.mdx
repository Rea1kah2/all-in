Markets test your emotions as hard as they test your analysis. Understanding your own biases often decides outcomes more than understanding charts.

## The biases that catch people out

<Term slug="fomo">FOMO</Term> pushes you to buy out of fear of missing a rally, which often means entering at high prices without proper analysis.

<Term slug="loss-aversion">Loss Aversion</Term> makes the pain of a loss feel sharper than the pleasure of a gain, so many investors hold losing positions far too long while hoping price returns.

<Term slug="confirmation-bias">Confirmation Bias</Term> leads you to seek only the information that supports what you already believe, and to dismiss evidence that contradicts it.

## The antidote is process

The counterweight to momentary emotion is <Term slug="discipline">Discipline</Term>, sticking to the plan you set, including entry rules, stop loss, and targets.

Every AI Analysis result in this app ends with the same reminder, the analysis helps you think more clearly, the final decision stays yours. Healthy psychology is what makes that reminder actually work.
